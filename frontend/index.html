System Pipeline & Ingestion Architecture

How documents flow from RBI portals into temporal vector retrieval and grounded generation.

1 Discovery & Registration

Scrapes RBI listing tables, computes SHA-256 digests, deduplicates, and logs to SQLite (metadata_db.py).

2 Extraction & Chunking

PyMuPDF extracts text while strictly preserving page numbers. Embeds chunks with all-MiniLM-L6-v2 into Qdrant.

3 Temporal RAG & Verification

Applies as_of_date filtering, identifies candidate deletions, validates quotes on original PDF pages, and verifies LLM grounding.
